# 📖 UMS User Manual
## University Marketplace System — University of Nairobi

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Registration
1. Go to the UMS homepage
2. Click **Register**
3. Enter your **UoN student email** (`yourname@students.uonbi.ac.ke`)
4. Fill in your full name, phone number and password
5. Check your email for a **verification link**
6. Click the link to activate your account
7. Login with your credentials

> ⚠️ Only `@students.uonbi.ac.ke` email addresses are accepted

---

## 2. Browsing & Searching

- Use the **search bar** to find items by name
- Filter by **Category** (Electronics, Books, Clothing etc.)
- Filter by **Condition** (New, Like New, Used, Heavily Used)
- Click any listing card to view full details

---

## 3. Posting an Item

1. Click **+ Post Item** in the navigation bar
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** — clear, descriptive name
   - **Category** — select the most appropriate
   - **Condition** — be honest about the item's condition
   - **Price** — in Kenya Shillings (KSh)
   - **Description** — include all relevant details
3. **Capture Photos:**
   - Allow camera access when prompted
   - Capture at least the **Front View** (required)
   - Additional angles (Side, Back, Close-up) improve buyer trust
   - Each photo is **AI-verified** to confirm it's a real item
4. Click **🚀 Post Item**

### 💰 Platform Fee Notice
UMS charges a **5% platform fee** on all sales:

| Your Listed Price | Platform Fee | You Receive |
|---|---|---|
| KSh 500 | KSh 25 | KSh 475 |
| KSh 1,000 | KSh 50 | KSh 950 |
| KSh 2,000 | KSh 100 | KSh 1,900 |

> The fee is automatically deducted from your payout.
> Buyers always pay the full listed price.
> Fees fund platform maintenance and student welfare programs.

---

## 4. Buying an Item

1. Find an item you want
2. Click on it to view full details
3. Check seller contact info (visible to logged-in students only)
4. Click **💚 Buy Now**
5. Enter your **M-Pesa phone number**
6. An **STK Push** will be sent to your phone
7. Enter your **M-Pesa PIN** to complete payment
8. You'll see a confirmation with your **M-Pesa receipt number**

---

## 5. Enquiries

- On any listing, scroll down to **"Questions about this item"**
- Type your question and click **Ask Question**
- The seller will be notified and can reply
- Resolved questions are marked with ✅

---

## 6. Community Help

- Click the **💬 Community Help** button on the right side of any page
- Ask non-trade questions:
  - Lecturer locations
  - Lost & Found items
  - Room or timetable changes
  - Campus events
- Other students can reply and mark best answers

---

## 7. My Profile

- Click **My Profile** in the navigation bar
- You can:
  - Update your name and phone number
  - Change your password
  - Upload or change your profile photo
  - View and manage your listings
  - Mark items as sold
  - Edit or delete listings
  - View your enquiry history

---

## 8. Reporting

- On any listing, click **⚑ Report this listing**
- Select a reason:
  - Scam or fraudulent listing
  - Inappropriate content
  - Item already sold
  - Wrong category
  - Spam or duplicate
- Our admin team reviews all reports within 24 hours

---

## 9. Security & Safety Tips

- ✅ Only trade with verified UoN students
- ✅ Always use the M-Pesa payment system
- ✅ Meet in safe, public campus locations
- ✅ Inspect items before completing payment
- ❌ Never share your M-Pesa PIN with anyone
- ❌ Never pay outside the UMS platform
- ❌ Report suspicious listings immediately

---

## 10. Forgot Password

1. Go to Login page
2. Click **"Forgot password?"**
3. Enter your student email
4. Check your inbox for a reset link
5. Link expires in **1 hour**
6. Set your new password

---

## 11. Admin Panel (Admin Only)

Accessible at `/ums/admin/dashboard.php`:

- **Dashboard** — System stats including platform fees collected
- **Manage Users** — Suspend, activate or delete student accounts
- **Manage Listings** — Remove or restore listings
- **Reports** — Review and action reported content
